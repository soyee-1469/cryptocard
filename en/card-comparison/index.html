NOTES FOR USERS IN KOREA

1. Overseas issuers support different countries. Check the official country list before applying.

2. KAST's higher cashback assumes a $1,000–10,000 annual fee; Ether.fi's higher tiers assume ETHFI staking. Looking only at the rate misses the cost of keeping it.

3. Paying in KRW at Korean merchants adds an FX fee on USD-based cards. Abroad, choose local currency rather than dynamic currency conversion (DCC).

4. Balances at overseas exchanges or card accounts may count toward Korea's foreign financial account reporting. See Korea crypto tax 2027.

5. Globally, users who experienced repeated cashback changes are moving to function-focused cards.
