Using crypto cards in Korea — payment and ATM checklist

Most crypto cards are issued by overseas companies and use USD as the base currency. In Korea, check availability and the cost of KRW payments first.

As of 11 October 2026

Before you apply

1Supported countries

Is Korea on the official list, and is physical card shipping available?

2Identity verification (KYC)

Passport and proof of address may be required.

3Top-up asset and network

Check supported stablecoins and networks; wrong transfers cannot be reversed.

4FX fee on KRW payments

KRW payments are converted on USD cards (KAST 0.5–1.75%, Ether.fi 1%, Anytap 1% + $0.30 abroad).

5DCC abroad

Choosing KRW abroad can mean double conversion; local currency is usually better.

6ATM costs

KAST $3 + 2% (max $250 each), Anytap 2% min. $1 (max $1,500). Local ATM operator fees may apply.

TAX AND REPORTING

Korea's virtual asset gains tax is scheduled for 2027, and overseas exchange or wallet accounts may count toward foreign account reporting. Read the tax summary →

NOTE

Prepaid card balances and virtual assets are not covered by deposit insurance. Korea's Virtual Asset User Protection Act may not apply equally to overseas providers.
