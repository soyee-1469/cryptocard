Cashback: comparison and cautions

Cashback is not a guaranteed right but a promotion whose terms the provider sets and can change.

Maximum and actual cashback differ. Look at the tier that applies to you, the monthly cap, the cost of keeping the rate and the real value of the payout asset.

As of 11 October 2026

How to judge cashback

Look at real savings, not the headline rate.

  1. Who qualifies?Rates differ by tier, annual fee or staking.
  2. Which payments count?Check eligible and excluded transactions.
  3. What is the monthly cap?Spend above the cap earns nothing.
  4. What does it cost to keep?Annual fees, staking price risk and opportunity cost.
  5. How is it paid?USD or own token? Can it be withdrawn?
  6. Can it change any time?Check the reward change/termination clause.

Five reasons cashback can cost you

1. Cashback is funded by costs you already paid

Card providers earn from issuance, annual fees, top-up and conversion fees, FX spread, foreign payment and ATM fees. Cashback is a marketing cost returned from that revenue.

Getting 1.5% back on a KRW payment that carries a 1% FX fee leaves only 0.5% real gain.

2. Terms can change before you are paid

Rate cuts, new caps, abolished staking rewards, regional shutdowns and suspended referral commissions have happened repeatedly across the industry.

KAST moved from points/token rewards to USD cashback in 2026 — whatever its merits, it shows reward terms can change.

3. You get tied to a platform or token

Top rates assume large annual fees (KAST) or staking own tokens (Ether.fi). Price falls and opportunity cost of staked assets never appear in the rate table.

Cashback usable only inside one app has value only if you keep using that app.

4. Caps stop you earning more

KAST Standard earns only on the first $2,000 a month, so $5,000 of spend still yields $30. Ether.fi Core drops to 1% above $2,000 and 0.5% above $3,000.

5. Upkeep can exceed the reward

To recover KAST Premium's $1,000 annual fee at 2%, you must spend over $50,000 a year (about $4,200 a month). Below that, the free tier is better.

IMPORTANT

Comparison has limits, and cashback is ultimately funded by costs users have already borne.

The real FX spread, internal settlement rate, timing of rate changes and remaining promotion budgets cannot be verified from public data. Table figures are 'published terms', not a guarantee of what you will be charged.

Choose by your actual use, not the highest rate. Check the reward change clause, the payout asset's liquidity and the conditions for keeping the rate.
