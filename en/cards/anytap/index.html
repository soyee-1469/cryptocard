Pros and cautions

Pros

Objectively verifiable strengths

  • Published numeric limits make large payments easy to plan.
  • Card-to-card transfer, personal wallet and external withdrawal all supported.
  • No annual fee; simple fee structure.
Cautions

Calculate before deciding

  • No cashback.
  • 2% on top-up.
  • Issuance fees apply.
  • Low spenders may pay more overall than with a cashback card.
