Ether.fi Cash review — cashback bands and Borrow Mode risk

Ether.fi Cash is run by an Ethereum-based protocol and lets you pay by depositing assets or using them as collateral. We classify it as a cashback-focused card.

Pros and cautions

Pros

Objectively verifiable strengths

  • 3% on the first $2,000 even on Core.
  • Pay against assets without selling them.
  • Non-custodial Safe wallet structure.
Cautions

Calculate before deciding

  • Rate falls quickly above $2,000.
  • Higher tiers need ETHFI staking with token price risk.
  • Borrow Mode can be liquidated if collateral falls.
  • Top-up fees and limits not disclosed officially.
