Crypto card picks 2026 — choose by use case

There is no single 'number one' crypto card for everyone. This page shows which card to look at first for each purpose, with the reasons and the caveats given equal weight.

As of 11 October 2026 · Sources: KAST, Ether.fi and Anytap official materials; 2026 public reviews

How we judged

Before the size of the rate, we looked at these.

Disclosure

Are fees and limits published item by item?

Payout asset

Is cashback paid in USD/stablecoins or a volatile token?

Conditions

Does the rate need an annual fee, staking or a specific payment method?

Limits

Do monthly, per-transaction and ATM limits fit real use?

Money mobility

Card-to-card transfers, wallet deposits and external withdrawals?

Cost transparency

Picks by use case

If cashback comes first

KAST

Why we pick it

  • Cashback is paid in USD, not a volatile token.
  • The Standard tier has no annual fee and no staking.
  • No FX fee on USD payments.

Watch out for

  • Standard earns 1.5% only on the first $2,000 a month — max $30.
  • Higher rates (2%, 3%) need a $1,000 or $10,000 annual fee.
  • Non-USD payments carry a 0.5–1.75% FX fee.
  • Depositing volatile coins triggers a sell spread; ATM is $3 + 2%.
  • Custodial account; availability in Korea must be checked officially.

If limits and money movement come first

Anytap Card

Why we pick it

  • Published limits: $1,000,000/month and $20,000 per transaction on the physical card; unlimited top-up.
  • Card-to-card transfer (0.5%), free personal wallet, free external withdrawal.
  • No annual fee; no bank account needed.

Watch out for

  • No cashback.
  • 2% on top-up; 1% + $0.30 on foreign payments.
  • Issuance: 20 USDT virtual, 100 USDT physical.
  • For low spenders, total cost can exceed a cashback card.
Read the Anytap Card review →
If you use Ethereum DeFi assets

Ether.fi Cash

Why we pick it

  • 3% on the first $2,000 of spend on the Core tier.
  • Borrow Mode lets you pay against collateral.
  • Built on a non-custodial Safe wallet.

Watch out for

  • Rate drops to 1% above $2,000 and 0.5% above $3,000.
  • Higher tiers require ETHFI staking.
  • Borrow Mode can be liquidated if collateral falls.
  • 1% FX fee; top-up fees not disclosed officially.
