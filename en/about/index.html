Principles and disclosure

Crypto Cards Info does not sell crypto cards. We apply the following principles across the site so you can judge real costs and features yourself.

Last updated 11 October 2026

Information principles

Objectivity

No unconditional praise or criticism; strengths and limits get equal weight.

Official sources first

Official sites, terms, fee tables and notices, with dates and sources on fee data.

No unverified figures

We mark gaps as 'Check official source', 'Not disclosed' or 'Not provided'.

Ads separated from facts

Phrases like '0% fees' are shown apart from the actual conditions.

Card types

Colour coding marks cashback-focused vs function-focused design, not a ranking.

Update discipline

Only real changes are updated; dates are never refreshed just for show.

CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST DISCLOSURE

If the operator receives advertising fees, affiliate commissions or other economic benefits from a compared provider, we disclose the relationship here and in related articles, and label sponsored or affiliate content.

Regardless of any relationship, comparison items, risk notices and card-type classification follow the same standard.

CORRECTIONS

If a figure differs from official materials, send us the evidence (official URL, clause, notice date). We will verify, correct and update the date. Provider requests are handled on the same basis.
