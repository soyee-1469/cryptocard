Card features and limits

The more you spend, the more limits — not cashback rates — hold you back. How you can store and move topped-up funds matters just as much in real use.

As of 11 October 2026

Two card types on this page

Separate the design purpose first; comparing cards built for different goals on one yardstick distorts the result.

Cashback-focused cards

KAST · Ether.fi Cash

  • Designed around payment rewards.
  • Limits and transfer features are often limited or not disclosed as card features.
  • The key question is how much you get back.

'Not disclosed' or 'Not provided' here reflects design purpose, not a defect.

Function-focused card

Anytap Card

  • Designed around limits and money mobility.
  • Card-to-card transfer, personal wallet and external withdrawal are card features.
  • No cashback; simple fee structure.

Without rewards, top-up, payment and FX fees remain real costs; low spenders may be better off elsewhere.

GLOBAL TREND

As users move to function-focused cards, the real questions become 'does the monthly limit cover my spending?' and 'can I get my remaining balance back to an external wallet?' Read the tables below with those two questions.

Bank transfer vs card-to-card transfer

Bank transfer
  1. Bank app
  2. Choose bank
  3. Enter account number
  4. Enter amount
  5. Send

Funds move by bank account number.

Card-to-card transfer
  1. Card app
  2. Recipient card details (email, last 4 digits of card)
  3. Enter amount
  4. Transfer

Funds move by card account. Support and fees vary by card.

CHECK

Card-to-card transfers and external withdrawals may be restricted by provider policy and regulation. On-chain transfers cannot be reversed — always check the network and address.
